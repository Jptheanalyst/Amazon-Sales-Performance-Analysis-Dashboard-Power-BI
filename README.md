# Amazon-Sales-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI
## Project Purpose
To transform raw e-commerce transaction data into an interactive executive dashboard that evaluates year-over-year revenue trends from 2022-2023, regional performance, category performance, and payment channel preferences to drive data-backed business decisions for growth.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Jptheanalyst/Amazon-Sales-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI/blob/main/amazon_sales_dataset.csv">Amazon Sales Dataset</a>
- Dataset Retrieved from :https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aliiihussain/amazon-sales-dataset

## Key Contributions & Achievements
- End-to-End BI Development: Ingested and modeled a dataset covering $32.8M+ in revenue, 50K orders, and 150K units sold, creating custom DAX measures for KPIs including Revenue, Total Orders, Quantities Sold, and Average Order Value (AOV).
- Trend & Seasonality Analysis: Evaluated 2022–2023 monthly revenue dynamics to identify peak purchasing cycles, uncovering January 2023 as the top revenue month ($1.46M / 4.45% of overall sales).
- Category & Regional Insights: Visualized category performance and regional distribution, revealing balanced market share across 4 global regions and identifying Beauty ($5.55M) and Books ($5.48M) as top-grossing categories.
- Customer Behavior Analysis: Analyzed checkout preferences across 50K total orders, pinpointing Amazon Wallet (10,106 orders) and UPI as the primary payment channels to guide customer UX optimization.
